# FAQ

**Do I need to install Python or anything else?**
No. AutoCards uses only what ships with Anki 26.09.

**Does it work on AnkiDroid or AnkiMobile?**
Generation runs on the computer. The cards sync to your phone normally.

**Can it open Anki by itself in the morning?**
That's up to your operating system: a startup entry or a scheduled task that opens Anki. As soon as the profile opens, AutoCards generates the day's cards.

**How much does it cost?**
With `gpt-4o-mini`, about a few US cents a month for 10 cards a day. Edge audio is free. See [Services and costs](services-and-costs.md).

**Will it flood me with cards?**
No. It starts small (*Increase gradually*), pauses when your overdue reviews pile up, never makes up for missed days, and has a monthly spending cap.

**Can it make cards from a PDF or a web page?**
Not yet.

**The AI got a fact wrong.**
For sensitive topics, turn on **Approve before adding** and check each card before it enters your study. A stronger model also helps.

**Which language does the interface use?**
English and Portuguese, following Anki's language.
